# Licences of the files in this archive

Written by Varians. The records are licensed by the people who made them, on the terms of contribution of the installation that published them.

- **Transcriptions and editions** (transcription.json, edition.json and the files beside them): the licence each names in `licence`, fixed when it was first published: CC BY-SA 4.0 (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>). One whose `licence` is null has not been published under a licence; `rightsNote` adds what its maker says about rights.
- **Conjectures, reported readings and notes**: CC BY-SA 4.0 (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>), the licence of the installation's records, named in `licence` in work.json.
- **The registry** (work.json, segments.tsv, witness.json, pages.json, persons.json, bibliography.json, bibliography.bib, citations.json): CC0 1.0 (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>).
- **Images** (witnesses/*/images): each under the rights statement `rights` in pages.json, with the credit line `attribution` where the rights holder asks for one. Only images whose statement allows copying are in the archive; the others are named by their source.
