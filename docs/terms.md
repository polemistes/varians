# Terms of contribution

These terms say what happens to what you put into this installation of Varians, and what you promise in doing so. They are part of having an account here. When they change, the new version is shown to you before you next edit or publish.

## What you contribute stays yours

You keep whatever rights you have in your transcripts, editions, conjectures, reported readings and notes. Nothing in these terms transfers them to the operator of this installation or to anyone else.

## Publishing licenses your work

When you publish an edition or a transcript here, you license it, non-exclusively and for ever, under the data licence of this installation, which the page *Sources and licences* names and every published record names too. The conjectures, reported readings and notes you publish with a work are licensed in the same way. A record keeps the licence it was first published under; later changes to these terms or to the installation's licence do not change it.

Varians keeps the full history of every text. Publishing a text publishes its history with it: every change, who made it and when, back to the text you started from.

## The registry is shared

Works, witnesses, segments, reference schemes and bibliography entries describe what exists, and everyone here describes the same manuscripts and works with them. What you register in the registry is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, so that anyone may use, correct and extend it without conditions.

## Texts you start from

A text you bring in to start from (an earlier edition, a digital text from another project) must be in the public domain or under an open licence that allows it to be published here, changed, and published again under this installation's licence. Its history is published with your work, and with it the text as you brought it in. Say where it comes from under *Sources*.

## Images

Upload an image only when you may have it shown here, and give its rights statement truthfully, with the credit line its holder asks for. An image is copied into the archives and downloads only when its rights statement allows copying; otherwise only its source is named.

## What the installation does with your work

The operator stores your work and shows it as you choose: drafts to you and those you invite, published records to everyone. The operator keeps an archive of every work in open formats, which anyone may download as far as the records are public, and may make copies of the archives and the database to preserve them. Your name, and your ORCID iD where you give one, are shown with your work and written into its archive and its citations; your address is not.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you publish. Do not publish what you have no right to publish, or what breaks the law. The operator may hide or remove material that does, and may close an account that is used to do so.

## Changes to these terms

A new version of these terms is shown to you before you next edit or publish, and you can go on reading everything until you accept it. What you published under an earlier version stays under the licence it was published with.
